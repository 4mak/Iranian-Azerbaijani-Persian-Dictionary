# Iranian Azerbaijani–Persian Dictionary

[فارسی](README.md)

## About the Project

This project is a free and collaborative dictionary for the Iranian Azerbaijani language.

The aim of this project is to collect, document, and preserve Azerbaijani vocabulary and provide their meanings and explanations in Persian.

This dictionary endeavors to record everyday, literary, historical, native, and lesser-known words of Iranian Azerbaijani, helping to preserve a part of the linguistic heritage of this language.

## Entry Format

Each entry is written in the following format:

> ***Word and pronunciation***  
> *Meaning and description*

---

The primary focus of this dictionary is on the **Tabrizi dialect**.

Alongside the standard form of words, common forms used in everyday speech, abbreviations, and pronunciation variations are also documented to better reflect the living language of the people.

## Goals
- Create a comprehensive Iranian Azerbaijani–Persian dictionary
- Document and preserve the vocabulary of the Tabrizi dialect
- Provide a free resource for research and learning
- Preserve old and regional words
